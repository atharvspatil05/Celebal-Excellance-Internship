# Celebal Excellence Internship – Data Engineering

This repository documents my learning journey during the **Celebal Excellence Internship (CEI)** at **Celebal Technologies**, undertaken from **15 June 2026 to 15 August 2026**.

The internship provided a structured and hands-on environment to explore core concepts of **Data Engineering**, combining guided learning with practical implementation. Throughout the program, I worked on assignments, exercises, and project-based tasks designed to strengthen both technical knowledge and problem-solving abilities.

## Repository Contents

* Weekly assignments and submissions
* Practice exercises and hands-on implementations
* Mini-projects
* Notes and learning resources from the internship

## Key Learning Areas

* Data Engineering fundamentals
* Data processing and transformation techniques
* Database management and analytics
* Modern data tools and technologies
* Building scalable and practical data solutions

## Objective

The purpose of this repository is to showcase my progress, maintain a record of the work completed during the internship, and demonstrate the practical skills gained through real-world learning experiences and mentorship at Celebal Technologies.
